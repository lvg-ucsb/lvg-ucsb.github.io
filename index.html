Latent Variable Group

An interdisciplinary research group at UC Santa Barbara advancing latent variable and mixture modeling methods through research, collaboration, and quantitative methods training.

Training & outreach

Funded methods training initiatives

Through externally funded training programs, LVG has supported researchers in learning, applying, and sharing principled mixture modeling methods. Karen Nylund-Gibson served as principal investigator for both completed initiatives.

Mixture Modeling for Discipline-Based Education Researchers

Training and mentoring for STEM education researchers using mixture models to investigate heterogeneity in educational experiences and outcomes.
