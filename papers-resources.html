Mixture Modeling with MplusAutomation

Our Bookdown brings together tutorials, worked examples, and reproducible resources for latent class, latent profile, latent transition, and related mixture modeling approaches. It includes Mplus and R workflows, making it a useful starting point for students and applied researchers as well as a home for the companion materials linked below.
