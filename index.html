About LVG

The Latent Variable Group (LVG) is a collaborative research community based in the Department of Education at the University of California, Santa Barbara. We bring together faculty, graduate students, and researchers with diverse methodological and substantive interests who share a common interest in latent variable modeling. Our work focuses on advancing latent variable and mixture modeling methods and applying them to questions in education and the social and behavioral sciences. Through methodological and applied research, invited presentations, symposia, workshops, and collaborative discussions with scholars from UCSB and other institutions, we foster the exchange of ideas and support thoughtful applications of quantitative methods.
