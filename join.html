Join LVG

The Latent Variable Group brings together students and researchers interested in advancing and applying quantitative methods in education and the social and behavioral sciences. We welcome opportunities for methodological learning, research collaboration, and interdisciplinary exchange.

Graduate Students

Karen Nylund-Gibson is accepting graduate students for Fall 2027. LVG welcomes graduate students interested in quantitative methodology, latent variable and mixture modeling, and interdisciplinary research. Students can participate in research discussions, develop methodological expertise, and explore opportunities for collaboration and mentorship.

Prospective graduate students apply through the relevant UCSB graduate program. Participation in LVG is separate from graduate program admission. Prospective students are encouraged to explore our research and group members before reaching out.

Graduate Student Interest Form — Fall 2027

Prospective students interested in working with Karen Nylund-Gibson are welcome to introduce themselves using this form. This inquiry is not a graduate admissions application; applicants must also follow UCSB's official admissions process.

Please do not include sensitive personal information. Submissions are processed by Formspree.

Undergraduate Researchers

We welcome inquiries from motivated UCSB undergraduates interested in learning about quantitative methods and participating in research. Depending on ongoing projects and capacity, opportunities may involve reviewing literature, preparing data, helping with research organization, or supporting applied and methodological projects. Advanced statistical experience is not necessarily required.

Undergraduate Research Interest Form

If you would like to work with LVG, please tell us a little about yourself. Submitting this form expresses interest but does not guarantee an available position. Your information will be sent through Formspree to the group director.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in your response. Submissions are processed by Formspree.

Collaborate With Us

We welcome collaborations with researchers at UCSB and other institutions. Our group is especially interested in methodological and applied questions involving latent variable and mixture models, longitudinal analysis, and statistical methods in education and the social and behavioral sciences.

If you are interested in presenting your research at an LVG meeting, discussing a methodological challenge, or exploring a collaboration, please contact knylund@education.ucsb.edu.
