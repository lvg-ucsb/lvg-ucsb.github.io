Director

Karen Nylund-Gibson

Professor of Education | Director, Latent Variable Group
Gevirtz Graduate School of Education · UC Santa Barbara

Karen Nylund-Gibson is a Professor of Education at the University of California, Santa Barbara, where she directs the Latent Variable Group (LVG). Her research focuses on the development and application of advanced quantitative methods, particularly latent variable and mixture modeling approaches, to better understand individual differences and patterns of change over time.

Her work bridges quantitative methodology and substantive research in education and the social and behavioral sciences. She collaborates with researchers across disciplines to address complex research questions and promote rigorous, thoughtful applications of statistical methods.

Karen is also passionate about quantitative methods education and training. She teaches graduate courses, mentors emerging scholars, and has led federally funded training initiatives designed to make advanced statistical methods more accessible to researchers.
